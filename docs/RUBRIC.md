## Learner objectives

After playing, the learner should be able to:

1. Explain that a training label is an example answer, and that an old hiring choice is not proof of job ability.
2. Follow an applicant through a learned decision tree and identify the examples supporting its answer.
3. Explain why removing one input may leave a related shortcut and why adding more records from the same process need not help.
4. Compare overall results with results for each city using counts and rates.
5. Explain why a repair should be tested on applicants kept out of training and tuning.

## Honest limits

Applicants are synthetic. Readiness is deliberately simplified to skill at least 60 and experience at least 1 year. Credits, pool sizes, mission targets and the three-star thresholds are game rules. Predictions, learned questions and outcomes are computed from the active data and choices. The game does not establish fairness for real hiring or prove learning without peer evidence.
